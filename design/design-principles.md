# Design Principles

## 1. Core Visual Philosophy
The design language for the **IDSC Student Research & Article Showcase Hub** draws inspiration from modern editorial interfaces (like *Untitled UI* and *Essos*). It blends high-contrast typography, generous whitespace, warm neutral backdrops, and subtle glassmorphic image overlays to create an elevated, student-centered directory that feels proud, professional, and authentic to the **Infotech Development Systems Colleges – Ligao City, Albay** community.

## 2. Key Design Pillars

### 2.1 Editorial Clarity & Scannability
* **Bold Serif/Sans Display Headlines:** Use clean, structured visual hierarchy where titles stand out immediately.
* **Frosted Glass Overlays:** As seen in modern blog showcases, cards utilize semi-transparent backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) over imagery for high-legibility text overlays (author names, dates, category tags).

### 2.2 Tactile & Subtle Elegance
* **Soft Earthy & Warm Neutrals:** Move away from harsh pure whites (`#FFFFFF`) or cold blues in favor of warm beige (`#FAF8F5` or `#F4F0EA`) framing containers paired with stark dark charcoal typography (`#18181B`).
* **Pill-Shaped Controls:** Rounded pill buttons (`border-radius: 9999px`) for primary actions ("Get Started", "Submit Blog", "Login") and category tag filters (*Artificial Intelligence*, *Information Technology*, *Agriculture*).

### 2.3 Purposeful Motion & Micro-Interactions
* **Card Lift & Zoom:** Subtle hover effects (`transform: translateY(-2px)`, subtle image scale `1.03`) to indicate interactability.
* **Arrow Indicators:** Actionable link elements feature arrow badges (`↗`) to clearly signal external navigation (opening the student's personal blog in a new tab).
